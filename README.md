# FitCore Gym Landing Page

A modern, responsive landing page for **FitCore Gym**, showcasing their premium fitness facilities, expert trainers, group classes, nutrition guidance, and membership options. Designed to engage visitors and encourage them to join the gym.

---

## Features
- Hero section with call-to-action  
- Why Choose FitCore Gym section  
- Premium Equipment, Expert Trainers, Flexible Hours highlights  
- More Than Just a Gym: Group Classes, Nutrition Guidance, Recovery Zone  
- Testimonials from members  
- Statistics: Active Members, Expert Trainers, Group Classes, Access Hours  
- Contact info and social media links  


---

## Technologies Used
- HTML5  
- CSS3
